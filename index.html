What happens when the road stops calling?

Long Way Home follows Ren Collins, a perennial traveler who discovers that the boldest adventure is committing to one place, one person, and a mission bigger than self. The book blends memoir, reportage, and practical guidance for anyone feeling the ache between freedom and foundation.

  • Navigate the emotional whiplash of slowing down after years on the move.
  • Design a rooted life that still leaves room for curiosity and autonomy.
  • Trade a personal brand for deep community and purposeful work.

The journey inside the pages

Part I — Leaving the Loop

Recognise the burnout behind the glamorous feed. Learn how to hear the internal signals that it is time to pause.

Part II — Rooting Down

Practical frameworks for choosing a home base, cultivating relationships, and honoring commitments when restlessness hits.

Part III — Living for More

Move beyond self-optimization toward work and impact that uplift the people and places you now call home.
